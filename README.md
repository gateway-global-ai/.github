# Gateway Global AI

**Architecting the Conscious Machine**

Welcome to the forefront of a new era in artificial intelligence. Gateway Global AI is not just building AI agents; we are pioneering the cognitive architecture for machines that think, remember, and relate.

## 🧠 Our Core Belief

The next leap in AI isn't about larger models—it's about deeper minds. We are integrating the foundational principles of human psychology—memory, personality, belief systems, and contextual understanding—into practical, enterprise-ready AI systems.

## 🎯 Why We're Different

While most AI focuses on task completion, we focus on **cognitive continuity**. Our work in temporal knowledge graphs, adaptive memory systems, and personality frameworks (like DISC) allows us to create AI that doesn't just answer a query—it understands the journey, learns from each interaction, and builds a persistent, evolving relationship with the user.

## 🚀 Our Mission

To bridge the gap between human intelligence and artificial capability. We develop B2B AI platforms and agents that offer:

- **Loyal, Realistic Digital Persons**: AI with consistent personality and values
- **True Conversational Memory**: Systems that recall and reason across time
- **Cost-Efficient, Sovereign Architecture**: Moving beyond expensive API dependencies to controlled, scalable, and private implementations

## 🏗️ Gateway Platform

Our unified, enterprise-grade TypeScript platform that consolidates:

- **AI Agents**: Memory-enabled agents with DISC personalities
- **MCP Server**: Model Context Protocol with extensible tools
- **Admin Panel**: React-based management interface
- **Core Tools**: Gemini Search, Google Places, Google Maps, Google Workspace, Identity, Telephony
- **Infrastructure**: Firebase, Vertex AI, environment separation (dev/stag/prod)

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

**Mandatory Technologies:**
- TypeScript (strict mode) - No Python or other languages
- React 18+ with Vite for frontend
- Node.js 20+ LTS for backend
- Pre-approved dependencies only

**Core Services:**
- Google Gemini AI & Vertex AI
- Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage)
- Redis for caching
- Vector databases for embeddings
